A Canadian inspector climbs up to count some barrels. One shifts under his foot. What the hell was supposed to be in that barrel for a single wobble to kick off an eighteen-million-dollar investigation?

Quebec, twenty twelve. This place holds a strategic reserve of maple syrup. Your mum has a strategic reserve too. It’s the good jam. Touch it and find out.

Here’s the logic. Nobody got the fucking trees to sign a supply contract. One year there’s sap everywhere. Next year? Sorry, mate. I’m a tree. So they store the surplus.

Rows and rows of identical barrels. The paperwork looks immaculate. Barrel here? Yep. Another barrel? Yep. Now try doing an inventory of what the fuck isn’t in them.

The thieves got roughly three thousand tonnes of syrup. Some barrels were empty. Others held water. Imagine writing the tasting notes. Crystal clear. Delicate on the tongue. Lingering notes of felony.

The haul was worth about eighteen million Canadian dollars. After most heists you change the locks. These guys also need somebody who can tell breakfast from fucking plumbing.

Now when a diner serves two pancakes with a single drop of syrup, I wonder. Are they being generous, or did Accounting finally approve the withdrawal?
