Eight people dead. Four homes. But three young children found at the scenes survived. Who could do this?

Ohio, April twenty-sixteen. Eight members of the Rhoden family and their loved ones were killed in a single night. Most were asleep. Investigators examined revenge and drug-related theories. Arrests would take two and a half years.

One important clue was a shoeprint. It was linked to inexpensive sneakers sold at Walmart. The evidence included a receipt for two pairs and store surveillance. The buyer was caught on camera two weeks before the murders. Who was this woman?

Angela Wagner. Her family knew the victims well. Her son Jake later pleaded guilty to all eight murders. Testimony would reveal the motive, changing the entire story.

A custody dispute over a little girl. Her father, Jake, wanted to take her from her mother. Eight lives, for control of a child. After the murders, he actually filed for custody. The girl's mother could no longer object.
