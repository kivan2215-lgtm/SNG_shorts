How do you test a warship? In Sweden they put thirty men on deck and told them to run from side to side.

Three runs in, the ship was rolling so badly they stopped the test. And the ship? Fuck it. They let it sail.

Stockholm, sixteen twenty-eight. A brand new warship called Vasa. Two gun decks. Carved lions. Apparently, if a ship looks expensive enough, physics gets embarrassed and looks away.

A gust of wind caught the sails. The ship heeled over. Water poured through the open gunports. Total distance from glorious departure to the bottom? About thirteen hundred metres.

An inquiry followed. Nobody was punished. Thirty guys tested the ship. Turns out the ship was testing the whole damn system. Does your boss also think stopping a test means you've passed it?
