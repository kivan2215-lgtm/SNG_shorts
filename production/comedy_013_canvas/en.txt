A Danish museum opens a crate. Then another. Imagine being the accountant. More than half a million kroner is somewhere between “fragile” and “this side up.”

The museum lent artist Jens Haaning actual banknotes. He was supposed to frame the cash to show what a year’s income looked like in Denmark and Austria. Then give it back. So far even the accountant gets the fucking metaphor.

Back to the crates. No banknotes. Two empty frames. There is a title, though. Take the Money and Run. The man sent in his fucking excuse and they had to hang that up too.

And the museum exhibited it. Imagine being the security guard. Your entire job is making sure nobody takes anything. How the fuck do you check at the end of your shift?

When the exhibition closed, the museum asked for the cash back. This is where art and accounting part company. In art, an absence can be deeply meaningful. In accounting, it’s a fucking discrepancy.

In twenty twenty-three a court ordered him to pay back nearly half a million kroner. His fee was deducted. Which means he actually got paid for the work.

That’s the bit that kills me. I’ve been producing fuck all at work for years. Never occurred to me to sign it.
