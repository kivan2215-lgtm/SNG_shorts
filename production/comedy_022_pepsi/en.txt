Pepsi put a price tag on a fighter jet. Then some bastard did the maths. Never put numbers in a joke if you sell to people with calculators.

Nineties Pepsi ad. A teenager parks a Harrier outside school. Seven million Pepsi Points. Finally, a loyalty programme with a fucking air force.

John Leonard reads the rules. Extra points cost ten cents each. Suddenly the Pentagon looks like it’s been shopping retail.

He sends fifteen actual points and a cheque for seven hundred thousand and eight dollars and fifty cents. Ten bucks for shipping and handling. On a fucking jet.

Pepsi returns the cheque and sends coupons. Imagine opening that envelope. Good news, sir. Your air force has been downgraded to refreshments.

Leonard sues. Now a federal judge has to watch a soda commercial and establish, for the record, how fucking obvious a joke is.

The judge rules no reasonable person would understand the ad as a real offer. Pepsi wins. The man read the fine print. Legally, that was the least reasonable thing he did all day.
