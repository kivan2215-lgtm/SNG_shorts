Most people on a plane want water, a blanket, or somebody’s fucking knee out of their back. Elvis wanted a federal narcotics badge. He wrote to Nixon from his seat. Imagine pressing the call button and asking if the president is working this flight.

December nineteen seventy. He shows up at the White House gates with a letter for Richard Nixon. The return address is his hotel. Imagine giving the president of the United States until fucking checkout.

Elvis offers to help fight drugs. Young people trust him, he says. Also, he’d like a federal narcotics badge. Incredible cover letter. No salary expectations, but he’d like the merch up front.

And the damn thing works. Elvis walks into the Oval Office in velvet, sideburns out to here, belt buckle big enough for Nixon to check his fucking teeth in. Undercover as what, exactly? You can buy this bastard’s police sketch in a record shop. Comes with twelve songs.

Then the Beatles come up. According to the meeting memo, Elvis thought they were a bad influence. He went in for a badge and found time to report a rival band to the president. Efficient little bastard.

Nixon tells his people to get him the badge. Elvis gets it. No entrance exam. Just a handwritten note and a chat with the one bloke in the building who can approve absolutely anything.

Now look at the photograph. Nixon has the face of a man who still has to explain to Accounts which budget line covers Elvis fucking Presley.
