# SNG Comedy002 — Mars Climate Orbiter

## Facts and qualifications
Primary source: NASA, Mars Climate Orbiter Mishap Investigation Board Phase I Report, 10 November 1999, executive summary pp6–7 and root cause p16.
https://llis.nasa.gov/llis_lib/pdf/1009464main1_0641-mr.pdf
Downloaded original retained in research/report.pdf; page16 excerpt shown in video.

Launch11 December1998; last signal23 September1999. Roughly nine-month cruise. Data for small thruster impulses from ground software were delivered in lbf-s instead of required N-s; downstream ground navigation processing assumed the specified metric units. This was NOT merely meters versus feet, NOT a claim that two onboard computers used different units. Conversion factor is approximately4.45. It applies to impulse/effect estimation, NOT a claim that the whole orbit or altitude was off by4.45 times.
Trajectory estimates accumulated error. Passage was much lower than intended. The primary report allows atmospheric destruction OR subsequent escape into heliocentric space; narration says mission lost and no explosion is depicted. Official later summary says burned up, but this preview avoids treating that as uniquely established by the initial report.
The report also identifies verification/testing and team communication problems. No individual employee scapegoat; no exact monetary loss claim; no crew or human deaths.
