# SNG 025 — Bluebelle / Terry Jo Duperrault

Research and production: 10–11 November 2026. Two separate portrait previews, RU and EN.

## Historical sources

1. [TIME, “The Sea: The Bluebelle’s Last Voyage”, 1 December 1961](https://content.time.com/time/subscriber/article/0,33009,938800,00.html), [page 2](https://content.time.com/time/subscriber/article/0,33009,938800-2,00.html). Contemporary reporting: Harvey’s account, the discovery of Terry Jo, his reaction and death, and her later testimony. The publicly accessible text was used; no access restriction was bypassed.
2. [LIFE, 1 December 1961, pp. 30–39](https://books.google.com/books?id=AVQEAAAAMBAJ&pg=PA30). Public page images: the float, the Captain Theo crew, hospital transport, Terry in hospital, the yacht, her family, Julian Harvey and recovery. The captions were checked to identify the subjects. Page 38, which contains the captain’s burial and other images, was not used.
3. [The Oregon Statesman, 22 November 1961, front page](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn85042470/1961-11-22/ed-1/seq-1/). AP report “Court Blocks Try to Get Capt. Harvey’s Papers”. Contemporary evidence that the Coast Guard was investigating an apparent mass murder, that Terry’s testimony implicated Harvey, and that he died before her detailed account. The video shows an actual crop, followed by an identified summary of the report.
4. [Wikimedia Commons newspaper photograph, 23 November 1961](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Terry_Jo_Dupperault_Captain_Theo_November_1961.jpg). Terry Jo holding a drawing of the rescue vessel. Low-resolution original used as a complete image; no generated facial details.
5. [Bluebelle history and source references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluebelle_(ship)). Secondary chronology cross-check: sinking 12 November, Harvey rescued 13 November, Terry rescued 16 November after approximately 82 hours, Harvey died 17 November. The public TIME and AP accounts independently corroborate the crucial sequence.
6. [Memoir excerpt published by TODAY](https://www.today.com/popculture/orphaned-sea-tere-now-tells-story-wbna36964672). First-person recollections used to check the cabin, deck, the captain’s behavior and escape onto the cork float.

The supplied [Coast Guard Proceedings PDF, April 1963](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/DCO%20Documents/Proceedings%20Magazine/Archive/1963/Vol20_No4_Apr1963.pdf) returned an access-denied HTML page. It was not used as a read source and is not shown in the video. Getty previews were not used.

## Fact decisions and chronology

| Claim | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Bahamas, 1961; family with three children on Bluebelle | Retained. LIFE family photograph shows four family members; Terry is pictured separately later. |
| Captain rescued in the morning | Changed to “the next day”; the commonly documented rescue time is around midday on 13 November. |
| Storm, broken mast and fire | Explicitly presented as the captain’s account. The dramatic storm scene is marked Reconstruction. |
| Eleven-year-old girl found on a small float | Retained; actual archival rescue photograph and crew photograph. |
| Almost four days without food or fresh water | Retained; approximately 82 hours shown as an approximate figure. |
| Harvey died after hearing of her survival, before her detailed testimony | Retained; the date graphic separates rescue (16 Nov), his death (17 Nov), and later testimony. No method or graphic depiction. |
| Terry found mother and brother on the exposed deck | Corrected to the cabin, consistent with her account. No bodies are depicted. |
| Calm sea and no fire | Retained as Terry’s account, contrasted with the captain’s claim. |
| Murder and deliberate sinking | Attributed to investigators; no claim of a trial or conviction. |
| Insurance motive | Excluded; no unproved motive presented as fact. |

- The cross-section is an explicitly schematic explanation of cabin → stairs → deck → float, not an authentic yacht blueprint. Movement and water are animated; the camera does not shake or zoom.
- Archival photographs remain still photographs. No AI-generated facial repair or fake archival motion is applied. A few portraits recur only for a clearly identified evidence comparison or family identification.
- Music, pulse and atmospheric sound are synthesized in `build.py`. No downloaded copyrighted music and no imitation of an authentic rescue recording.
