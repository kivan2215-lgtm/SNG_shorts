The captain of a sunken yacht was found alive. He said a terrible storm had killed his passengers. But days later, a discovery at sea would destroy his story.

The Bahamas, nineteen sixty-one. A family with three children is sailing aboard the Bluebelle. One night, the yacht sinks. The next day, Captain Julian Harvey is rescued. He claims the mast broke, a fire started, and everyone else was lost.

But on November sixteenth, sailors spot a tiny float. On it is eleven-year-old Terry Jo. Nearly four days without food or fresh water! When the captain learns she is alive, he leaves his questioning. He returns to a motel. Soon afterward, he dies by suicide. Before Terry is well enough to tell investigators her full story.

And her account changes everything. The sea that night was calm. There was no fire. Screams woke her. Upstairs in the cabin, she found her mother and brother lying dead. When Terry approached the captain, he shoved her away. The yacht began to sink. She managed to release a small life float.

Investigators concluded this was a crime disguised as a shipwreck. Harvey had killed the passengers and sunk the yacht. But there was one thing he had not planned for. The girl left alone in the ocean survived. And exposed his lies.
