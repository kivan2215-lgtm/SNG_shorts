Title: The Captain Said No One Survived. Then They Found a Raft

In November 1961, the Bluebelle sank near the Bahamas. Its captain described a storm and a fire. Days later, sailors found a tiny life float carrying eleven-year-old Terry Jo Duperrault. Her account changed the investigation completely.

Based on contemporary reporting from 1961. The video combines archival photographs with labeled artistic reconstructions.
