# The Cadaver Synod — Rome, 897

## History

- Formosus died in April 896. About nine months later Stephen VI convened the Roman synod and had his predecessor’s body exhumed, dressed in papal vestments and seated on a throne. A deacon answered for Formosus. [Encyclopaedia Britannica, Formosus](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Formosus)
- One charge was an unlawful transfer from the episcopal see of Porto to the see of Rome. A bishop’s transfer between sees was then forbidden. Perjury was also alleged. The synod found Formosus guilty, invalidated his election and annulled his acts, including ordinations. Stephen himself had been made bishop of Anagni by Formosus. [Encyclopaedia Britannica, Stephen VI (or VII)](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Stephen-VI-or-VII)
- Later in 897, Pope Theodore II restored Formosus’s ordinations and had him ceremonially reburied in Saint Peter’s. John IX subsequently condemned Stephen’s synod. [Encyclopaedia Britannica, Formosus](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Formosus)
