First guy into the Olympic stadium. The crowd goes wild. Funny thing is, if his car hadn't broken down, he wouldn't even be fucking running.

St. Louis. Olympic marathon. Thirty-two starters. Heat, dust, and Fred Lorz gets cramps. He drops out and catches a ride. Bad day for his legs. Tremendous day for his ass.

Then the car breaks down. So Fred starts running again. Past the crowd. Into the stadium. All those people see is the finish. Holy shit, this champion looks fresh! Of course he does. His ass handled the hard part.

Witnesses spotted the ride. Lorz was disqualified. Thomas Hicks was the real winner. But check the photo: after the finish, Hicks is sitting in a car too. Same damn idea. One got a ride afterwards. The other was a little early.

A year later Lorz won the Boston Marathon. On foot. Imagine being that good at running, and a century later everyone remembers that time you were fucking magnificent at sitting down.
