The plane lands. The passengers hear the name of the city and laugh. Good one, captain. Then the crew asks for a show of hands. Who was expecting Düsseldorf? Every fucking hand goes up.

London, March twenty nineteen. These people bought tickets to Germany. They landed in Scotland. Imagine asking a cabbie how far it is to your hotel and he starts by asking what currency you packed.

Here’s the beautiful part. The pilots knew exactly where they were. So did air traffic control. Right runway. Lovely landing. A remarkable number of people got absolutely everything right. Welcome to fucking Edinburgh.

The crew’s paperwork said Edinburgh. The tickets said Düsseldorf. Put those two sheets together in London and you save a whole fucking airport. Instead they checked the paperwork against a life-size sample of Scotland.

Who wants Germany? Up go the hands. What a fucking navigation system. Imagine the captain holding another vote. All in favour of Bali? Lovely. That’s still Edinburgh out the bloody window.

They refuelled and flew to Düsseldorf. Spare a thought for the poor bastard who swapped seats so his neighbour could see Germany. That guy’s on his second fucking country. You’re on your third close-up of the drinks trolley.

I used to think the dumbest question after landing was whether anybody had forgotten their hand luggage. Turns out there’s a better one. Should we even get the fuck off?
