# SNG 028 — Ken Rex McElroy / Skidmore

## Editorial and factual decisions
- Killing: 10 July 1981, outside D&G Tavern, Skidmore, Missouri; wife in pickup; dozens of witnesses.
- Different accounts give 30–60 people; use “dozens,” never a definitive 40 or a precisely numbered crowd diagram.
- Trena accused a named man; other witnesses denied seeing the gunman. Do NOT say literally nobody identified anyone, do not portray any uncharged person as a proven killer.
- Two weapons are reported, hence “one of the gunmen” in the wife's account. No precise firing positions, bullets, shooter identities or reconstructed attack depicted.
- Grocer Ernest “Bo” Bowenkamp survived the 1980 attack. McElroy convicted in 1981, freed on bond pending appeal, threatened grocer again. No false acquittal claim.
- Number of police interviews omitted because supplied “more than 100” was not independently corroborated.
- No one charged; federal civil-rights investigation and grand juries did not produce indictments. Final moral question explicitly leaves murder unjustified.
