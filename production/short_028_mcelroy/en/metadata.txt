Title: Shot in Front of a Crowd. A Town Stayed Silent.

On July 10, 1981, Ken McElroy was killed outside a tavern in Skidmore, Missouri. Dozens of people were nearby. His wife said she recognized a gunman, but the investigations never led to charges. A true story about fear, silence, and the line between justice and vigilantism.
