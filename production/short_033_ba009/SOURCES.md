# SNG 033 — British Airways Flight 009

## Facts checked
- BBC, 15 April 2010, captain Eric Moody's account: https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/magazine/8622099.stm — 24 June 1982; Kuala Lumpur–Perth leg; unusual electrical glow and smoke; all four engines failed; repeated restart attempts; roughly 15 minutes without thrust; cooling deposits broke away; Jakarta landing; another engine shutdown; damaged windscreen. Spoken wording is our narration, not a fabricated cockpit recording.
- RNZ, 22 June 2022, captain and passenger accounts: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/national/469560/forty-years-on-passengers-remember-aviation-miracle-after-mt-galunggung-eruption — independent narrative corroboration; all aboard survived. Descriptions of a freefall are not repeated: the model shows a glide/descent.
- FAA AIM §7-6-10: https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap7_section_6.html — volcanic ash can be invisible at night and is not displayed on weather radar; engine flameouts, static discharge and cabin smoke are documented consequences. This historical video is not operating guidance for pilots.
- Secondary cross-check: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Airways_Flight_009 — aircraft G-BDXH, engine failure order 4,2,1,3 and second shutdown of engine 2. Do not repeat the unsourced “oxygen deprived” theory; describe disrupted airflow from deposits.

The script avoids conflicting passenger counts (247/248 in secondary accounts), exact restart altitude, an invented countdown, and verbatim reconstructed dialogue. Approximate 15-minute duration is attributed to contemporary reporting / captain's recollection, not an exact second-by-second reconstruction. The opening introduces failure, then an earlier warning sign; the dated account follows.

## Authored illustrative material
All aircraft, engine, cabin, radar, windscreen and runway scenes are code-built explanatory 3D models. They are not authentic accident footage, exact aircraft engineering drawings, actual cockpit instrument readings, or measured flight paths. No recreated radio recording or identifiable passenger portrait is used. Generic clothed adults use the MakeHuman CC0 mesh/rig data with licenses in `human/`; their positions are illustrative.

Cycles lighting and materials follow approved Squalus V4. 720×1280 model renders; 1080×1920 composition and typography. Moving model states are 30 fps over their authored two-second action followed by a still hold; overlays render at 30 fps. Unchanged background is cached only after full-frame comparisons. No optical-flow interpolation. Smoke/flow arrows/ash particles are explanatory animated overlays, not recorded evidence.

Voice: project-generated DmitryNeural (RU) and GuyNeural (EN), previous approved paragraph pacing. Music/effects are original procedural synthesis in build.py, not a copied commercial track. No new auditory review is claimed; technical checks are reported separately.
