# New story request and episode 035 production notes

User asks each new episode to improve intrigue, shock, graphic detail, animation and narration while retaining the established edit style. This is a goal, not a measurable promise of higher retention.

Use a real case with multiple sourced discoveries: initial danger, contradictory evidence, another event, delayed identification, established motive/outcome, discussion question. Do not invent a person's location or conduct merely to strengthen the hook. Episode 035 removed an unsupported suggestion that the husband was waiting on the ground.

Generated contact sheets can have unexpected aspect ratios. Inspect actual dimensions and preserve them in video composition; never force a square panel into a portrait rectangle by stretching faces. Check whole helmets, hands and other key action objects. Record unused panels separately from the timeline.

A field must look like soil, not wooden slats: continuous uneven terrain mesh, material variation and bump detail. Models must have meaningful movement and stable context. Explanatory geometry is not measured accident physics. Keep any essential scale caveat brief.

Authentic photographs can return for genuinely new information, but each important action should receive a distinct visual. Keep culprit portraits hidden until the spoken reveal. Preserve the RU/EN narrator preset and independently timed captions. Technical checks do not verify pronunciation by ear.
