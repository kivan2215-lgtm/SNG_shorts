Ryan tried to explain: his former roommate, Richie Carver, and Richie's father had come to the house. He had been shot. So you got shot first? Mm-hmm. And what happened? Did you fall on the ground? Yeah, I was trying to get up and could not. But officers did not immediately recognize the wound. The court decision describes him as disoriented and confused. Only after noticing a small hole beside his nose did police call paramedics.

A bullet fragment was lodged behind his left eye, in the temporal lobe of his brain. Swelling made surgery necessary. Ryan survived, but suffered a devastating injury. Investigators established that the attack happened on December twenty-third. Help arrived two days later. Richie Carver and his father, Larry, were both convicted.

But a conviction did not end Ryan's story. On January twentieth, two thousand sixteen, he died. He was only twenty-seven. And this recording leaves a question: why was a man with a severe facial injury treated as a suspect for so long before officers recognized his head wound?
