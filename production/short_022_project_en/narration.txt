Look at this photograph. Two friends at a party. But one already knows what the other has done. And soon, he will tell the police.

Florida, twenty-eleven. Seventeen-year-old Tyler Hadley invites friends over. Dozens of guests arrive. Music, laughter. Nobody sees his parents. Tyler says they are out of town.

But his friend Michael Mandell notices suspicious traces near the bedroom. Tyler pulls him aside and admits he killed his parents. Michael thinks it is a terrible joke. Then Tyler tells him to look inside the room.

Behind the door, beneath a pile of belongings, are the bodies of Mary-Jo and Blake Hadley. Hours before the party, Tyler killed them with a hammer. Then he invited guests into the same house. They were nearby all evening, without knowing.

Before leaving, Michael poses for a photograph with Tyler. The one you saw at the beginning. Michael later explained that he planned to contact police, and wanted one last photo with his friend. He reported what he knew that night. Tyler was arrested in the morning. Now look at the photograph again. Michael already knew the truth.
