Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds. Yes. Fucking birds. The humans were about to have a very awkward week.

Nineteen thirty two. Thousands of emus are trampling wheat. Farmers ask for help. Three military men arrive. Two machine guns. Ten thousand rounds. The birds have legs. Brilliant fucking matchmaking.

But the emus scatter into small groups. The soldiers cannot keep up. So they set an ambush by the water. A huge flock approaches. Finally, their big moment! The machine gun jams. The birds leave. Human intelligence is experiencing technical difficulties.

Fine. Put a machine gun on a truck. Now they can chase them! Except the ride is so rough, the gunner cannot shoot properly. The birds get away again. They ordered mobility. They got an ass massage. The troops are recalled. Then sent back. The problem remains.

Birds did die, so this was no bloodless victory. But the military operation ended, and the farmers still had emus. History remembers it as the Emu War. At what point should someone have admitted that the plan was complete bullshit?
