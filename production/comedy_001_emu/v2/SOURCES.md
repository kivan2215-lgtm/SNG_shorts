# SNG Comedy 001 — Emu War, November–December 1932

## Verified story / qualifications
- Military personnel were sent to Western Australian farms after emus damaged crops: three men, two Lewis guns, initial allocation10,000 rounds. The men were Major Meredith, Sergeant McMurray and Gunner O'Halloran. The script does not call this an entire deployed army or a formal declaration of war.
- Birds scattered in small groups; a water-side ambush failed when a gun jammed. A gun was mounted on a truck, which could not keep up effectively and whose rough ride prevented useful shooting.
- First withdrawal8November; renewed deployment13November (ministerial approval12November). Final recall10December. Our narration preserves both phases and never says soldiers immediately gave up forever.
- The cull killed birds; sources differ substantially on early totals, later totals are reported claims. No exact death toll is used. “The problem remained” refers to continuing emu damage and subsequent requests, not a claim of zero effect on crops.
- “War with birds” is the historical popular nickname. No fictional surrender, treaty, bird general, medal or military intelligence is presented as fact. Profane punchlines, the reboot indicator and matchup comparisons are original editorial jokes, not quotations.
