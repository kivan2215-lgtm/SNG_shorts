Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight emus, giant fucking birds whose entire defense budget consists of two legs. And somehow, this brilliant plan still managed to turn into a complete shitshow.

In nineteen thirty two, emus were trampling wheat, so farmers asked for help and got three military men with two machine guns and ten thousand rounds. The birds just had legs, but at least the bastards did not need permission to use them.

But the emus scattered into small groups, forcing the soldiers to discover that a target can, in fact, fuck off. So they set an ambush by the water: a huge flock approached, everything was ready, and the machine gun jammed. The birds left while human intelligence froze like a printer demanding a fucking cyan cartridge.

They mounted a machine gun on a truck to chase the feathery bastards, but the ride was so rough that the gunner could not shoot properly. The emus escaped again, and instead of a fighting vehicle, they had built a fucking vibrator on wheels. The troops were recalled, then sent back, but the problem remained.

Birds did die, so forget the internet fairy tale of a bloodless victory: the operation ended, and the farmers still had emus. The humans had weapons and ranks, while their opponents had feathers and absolutely no interest in this bullshit. At what point should somebody have admitted that the plan was crap?
