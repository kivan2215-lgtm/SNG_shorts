Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight emus, giant fucking birds whose entire defense budget consists of two legs. And somehow, this brilliant plan turned into a complete shitshow before the birds even realized there was a war.

In nineteen thirty two, emus were trampling wheat, so farmers asked for help and got three military men with two machine guns and ten thousand rounds. The birds just had legs, but at least the bastards did not need permission to use them.

But the emus scattered into small groups, forcing the soldiers to discover that a target can fuck off without submitting a request. So they set an ambush by the water: a huge flock approached, everything was ready, and the machine gun jammed. The birds left while human intelligence froze like a printer without cyan ink: black ink is full, but the fucking thing still refuses to work.

They mounted a machine gun on a truck to chase the feathery fuckers, but the ride was so rough that the gunner could not shoot properly. The emus escaped again, and instead of a fighting vehicle, they had built a fucking vibrator on wheels with government funding. The troops were recalled, then sent back, but the problem remained: a second date with failure was still a shit date.

Birds did die, so forget the internet fairy tale of a bloodless victory: the operation ended, and the farmers still had emus. The humans had weapons and ranks, while their opponents had feathers and zero interest in this bullshit. At what point do you admit the plan is crap instead of putting fucking wheels on it?
