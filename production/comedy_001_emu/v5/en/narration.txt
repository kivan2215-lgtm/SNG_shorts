Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight emus, giant fucking birds whose entire defense budget consists of two legs. And somehow, this brilliant plan became a shitshow. Who could have guessed the birds would not politely queue up to be shot?

But the emus scattered into small groups, forcing the soldiers to discover that a target can fuck off. What a breakthrough for people carrying guns! So they set an ambush by the water: a huge flock approached, everything was ready, and the machine gun jammed. The birds left while human intelligence froze like a printer without cyan ink. Two machine guns against birds, and fucking tech support is unavailable.

And guess what these fucking geniuses tried next? They mounted a machine gun on a truck to chase the feathery fuckers, but the ride was so rough that the gunner could not shoot properly. Holy shit, bumps make a truck bounce! The emus escaped again, and instead of a fighting vehicle, they had built a fucking vibrator on wheels with government funding. The troops were recalled, then sent back, but the problem remained: apparently one fuckup was not enough, so they ordered seconds.

Birds did die, so forget the internet fairy tale of a bloodless victory: the operation ended, and the farmers still had emus. The humans had weapons and ranks, while their opponents had feathers and zero interest in this bullshit. At what point do you admit the plan is crap instead of putting fucking wheels on it?
