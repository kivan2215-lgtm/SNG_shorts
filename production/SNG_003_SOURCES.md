## Facts

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Chowchilla_kidnapping (saved wiki.html).
- CBS News / 48 Hours, David Begnaud, March 18, 2023: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/chowchilla-bus-kidnapping-frederick-woods-survivor-i-felt-like-i-was-an-animal-going-to-the-slaughterhouse/ (saved cbs.html). Describes almost 12 hours in the vans, 16 hours in the buried trailer, mattresses, Michael Marshall and Ed Ray's escape, planned $5 million ransom never delivered.
- City of Chowchilla: https://www.cityofchowchilla.org/301/Edward-Ray---A-Local-Hero (saved city.html).

Corrections from supplied script: "eleven hours" became "almost twelve hours" in accordance with CBS eyewitness account. Sixteen hours is the approximate duration until escape, not a claim that they waited sixteen hours before attempting escape. Dates and victims: July 15–16, 1976; 26 schoolchildren and driver, all 27 survived. Demand planned, not paid. Victims may have suffered long-term trauma; survival is not presented as absence of harm.
