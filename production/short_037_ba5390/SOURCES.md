# SNG 037 — British Airways Flight 5390

Production date: 17 November 2026. Full RU/EN review previews, not user-approved finals. Sources below were accessed during production. Public accessibility is not treated as a licence. No actual in-flight photograph/recording of the event is claimed.

## Primary factual source

**UK Department of Transport / AAIB, Aircraft Accident Report 1/1992**, accident 10 June 1990; report signed January 1992, published February 1992. GOV.UK upload date 10 December 2014 is not the event/report date.
- Landing page: https://www.gov.uk/aaib-reports/1-1992-bac-one-eleven-g-bjrt-10-june-1990
- Report PDF: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5422faa7e5274a131400078d/1-1992_G-BJRT.pdf
- Appendix PDF: https://assets.digital.cabinet-office.gov.uk/media/5422faa8ed915d13710007d1/1-1992_G-BJRT_Append.pdf

Checked the actual scanned report pages, with OCR as a reading aid:
- Printed pp2–4: 81 passengers, 4 cabin crew, 2 pilots; Birmingham–Malaga; loss of left windshield at0733UTC while climbing through about17,300ft pressure altitude; attendant first grasps waist, colleagues assist, later captain held by ankles; landing Southampton0755UTC. Opening is a later state, not a claim ankles were the first grip.
- Printed p13: commander regained consciousness after landing; crew observed him kick his legs at roughly3000ft during descent. **Narration preserves this sign of life before landing.** Hospital photograph appears only after landing.
- Printed p12–13 and52: windshield replaced27h before flight;84of90bolts diameter0.026in smaller than specified (~0.66mm); other6correct diameter but0.1in too short(2.54mm). Diagram uses enlarged nominal proportions, not an assembly drawing.
- Printed pp52–55: missing independent final inspection; inadequate working practices and management/quality-audit oversight; not just a single faulty part with no systemic context. Discussion question reflects these findings, not an unsupported accusation.
- All87survived; no fatality. ~20minutes outside is rounded narrative duration: window failure0733 to landing0755=22minutes, with initial restraint/positions changing. No fake countdown.
