# SNG 026 — bilingual portrait Shorts

Run `python build.py ru` or `python build.py en` with ffmpeg/libass, Pillow and NumPy installed. Preserved voice.wav and words.json are sufficient to rebuild. voice.py preserves established voices; pace.py trims long silence after fresh synthesis. visuals.py renders original diagrams and archival layouts at 1080x1920. No camera shake or repeated generated panels. See SOURCES.md and sources.json for provenance. Final preview duration: RU about 82 seconds; EN about 72 seconds. The name and portrait of Hupp are withheld until the final reveal.
