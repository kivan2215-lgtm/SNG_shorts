# SNG Shorts 031 — Mary Vincent

RU and EN independent preview cuts. 1080×1920, 30 FPS, H.264/yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz stereo, faststart MP4. Accepted voices DmitryNeural and GuyNeural; sentence pauses shortened without speeding speech. Narrative, archival identities and output-language captions preserved in each language folder.

Build with `python build.py ru` and `python build.py en`. Completed narration and word timings are included; no need to re-synthesize. TTS dependency used: edge-tts 7.2.3 with the environment CA bundle. Full decode and audio/stream/caption checks in QC.json.

The opening photo is intentionally returned to when the testimony is explained. Dates separate the 1997 arrest from the 1998 court appearance and the 1999 advocacy event. The graphic attack is conveyed verbally and through neutral location illustrations without depicting violence against a child. Sources, photo credits, permissions basis and factual corrections are in SOURCES.md.
