# SNG 036 — John Darwin

Checked 16 November 2026. Real documentary story. Publication and capture dates are separate. The user has reported permission to publish photographs throughout this project; that is recorded as user-reported permission, not an independently verified open licence or a claim that public access means public domain.

## Fact verification

1. BBC News, **Canoe fraud couple sent to jail**, 23 July 2008: https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/england/tees/7520803.stm
   Court reporting: 2002 disappearance; wife knew; life insurance and other payments; sons were unaware and were described by the judge as the real victims. John admitted fraud. Sentences: John six years three months, Anne six years six months. These are 2008 sentences, not claims about current imprisonment. No claim they served the entire sentence.
2. BBC News, **Timeline: 'Missing' canoe man**, updated 23 July 2008: https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/england/7133059.stm
   Disappearance 21 March 2002; inquest April 2003; return 1 December 2007. Five years is a rounded duration (actually more than five and a half). Reports differ on when the wrecked kayak was recovered, so narration deliberately says only that it was found, not a specific day.
3. Guardian, Matthew Weaver and agencies, **Woman found canoeist photo via Google**, 6 December 2007: https://www.theguardian.com/uk/2007/dec/06/ukcrime.matthewweaver
   A member of the public found the real Panama photograph online and sent it to police and the Daily Mirror. Photograph from July 2006, before December 2007 return. Not a fabricated search screenshot. The photo was not the first or only police lead: financial inquiries had already begun.
4. Guardian, Lee Glendinning, **Detectives suspected Darwin was alive**, 23 July 2008: https://www.theguardian.com/uk/2008/jul/23/canoe.ukcrime5
   Investigation predated reappearance; police concluded the sons had not known. We do not claim the photograph single-handedly began or solved the investigation.
5. BBC News, **Hidden door used by John Darwin**, 23 July 2008: https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/england/7520151.stm
   Door behind wardrobe connected the marital home at number three The Cliff with bedsits at number four. Our model explains this spatial connection; it is NOT the police's original model, NOT a measured floor plan, and not evidence of exact furniture, dimensions or walking movements.
6. BBC News, Duncan Kennedy, **Trail of mistakes leads to Panama**, 23 July 2008: https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/england/7292469.stm
   Genuine Panama agency photo, summer 2007 photograph of John at land in Panama. Agency adviser Mario Vilar is the third person in the photo. He is not identified as an accomplice; no such claim is made.
7. BBC News, **Canoe fake death wife Anne Darwin: I'll feel guilt until I die**, 5 October 2016: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-tyne-37551735
   Republishes case photos with credits. Her later reconciliation with sons is outside the short's 2008 endpoint. We do not claim estrangement lasted forever.
8. Guardian / Press Association, **The mystery of John Darwin**, 5 December 2007: https://www.theguardian.com/uk/2007/dec/05/ukcrime
   Contemporary John photograph AFP/Getty Images. Early reporting is cross-checked against 2008 trial reporting.
