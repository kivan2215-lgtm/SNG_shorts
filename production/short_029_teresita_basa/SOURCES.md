# SNG 029 — sources and provenance

## Documented facts

- [The Washington Post, 17 September 1978](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1978/09/17/voice-from-the-grave-evokes-a-murder-trial/13b6d805-2413-4015-b70a-b9b28ab5d40d/): contemporary account of the murder investigation, Jose and Remibias Chua's account, the lead naming Allan Showery, and the hospital connection. The supernatural account is attributed to the couple; it is not presented as a verified phenomenon.
- [DNAinfo Chicago, 13 September 2016](https://www.dnainfo.com/chicago/20160913/edgewater/did-teresita-basa-solve-her-own-murder-true-life-ghost-story-still-haunts/): pearl ring and jade pendant recovered from Showery's girlfriend, the hung jury in the first trial, the later guilty plea and 14-year sentence. Discusses an ordinary path by which information might have reached Remibias; the actual source is unknown.
- [Crime Junkie case article](https://crimejunkiepodcast.com/captured-allan-showery/): photograph identification for Teresita Basa and Allan Showery.
