- Philip Oltermann, The Guardian, 6 August 2024, first-person interview: "A bolt of lightning struck my plane – and I plunged 3,000m into a rainforest". URL retained in guardian.html, source linked by flight.html. This account confirms lightning, approx 3000m, 17 years old, 24 Dec 1971, broken collarbone, glasses lost, swollen eye, one trainer, 11 days, advice to follow running water, forest canopy obscuring rescuers, and 92 on board / sole eventual survivor.

Correction to the supplied scenario: the rescuers were local woodcutters/hunters, not specifically fishermen. Exact shelter discovery day varies between sources, so narration does not insist on "tenth day, next morning". Does not claim the other 91 all died instantly. Does not assert a proven biomechanical explanation of survival, invent animal attacks or graphic injury details. River principle diagram is NOT a recovered route map. Rescue is shown only in last 15 seconds; survival of the fall is the requested opening hook.
