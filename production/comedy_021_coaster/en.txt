The guy beside you on a roller coaster is clutching his backpack. His phone in there? Fuck no. It’s a kidney. And it has its own bloody itinerary.

Doctor David Wartinger heard something odd from patients. They’d ridden a roller coaster at Disney World and passed a kidney stone. Most people would say, hell of a coincidence. A researcher wants to know where they sat.

He and a colleague put real stones inside a clear kidney model, packed it in a backpack and took it on a coaster in Florida. Other people come home with sunset photos. These two had a renal expense report.

Twenty rides. After each one they checked where the stones had gone. Everyone else is screaming for joy. Your whole fucking afternoon depends on the emotional journey of some gravel.

Up front, stones cleared the chamber in four out of twenty-four trials. At the back, twenty-three out of thirty-six. Same kidney. Apparently you get better service in the arse end of the train.

It was a model. Nobody was prescribing Disney tickets. But imagine the insurance claim for twenty rides. Sorry, was the fucking kidney eligible for a child fare?

They won an Ig Nobel Prize. Now that’s career development. First you carry a backpack onto a roller coaster. Then the backpack carries your career.
