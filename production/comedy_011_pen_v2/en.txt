How do you take a free pen and make it a diplomatic incident? Watch his hands. Especially the bit where he runs out of hands.

Chile, two thousand eleven. The Chilean president is giving a speech. Beside him, Czech president Václav Klaus opens a presentation case. Inside is a pen with a blue stone. You can practically see foreign policy leave the man’s body. Fuck me. That’s coming home.

He picks it up. Both hands disappear beneath the table. Adjusts his jacket. Does up a button. Both hands come back empty. I’ve nicked a third meatball from my mother-in-law with less choreography. And she watches the fucking meatballs.

Then he spots the open case. Gently closes the lid. Beautiful, Václav. Perfect crime. Just you, the pen, and a bloke whose entire fucking job is to point a camera at you.

Czech TV airs it. The internet adds arrows and heist music. Then Chile has to explain that visiting guests are allowed to keep the pens.

It was a fucking gift. He accepted it so suspiciously I put my own pen back. I’m at home. I bought this bastard.

Back in Czechia, people suggested mailing pens to the president. Democracy in action. The people saw a need. Mostly because somebody got it on fucking camera.
