## Editorial choices
The trap sequence is explicitly attributed to police theory, not eyewitness fact. Diagrams are labeled illustrative and are not surveyed floor plans or exact trap designs. Bodies are not pictured. Automobile parts replaces a simplistic claim of a complete car. The starvation statement includes heart disease. English uses ten feet, Russian approximately three meters.
Generated scenes: one narrow passage, one piano-and-books room, one food still life. Each generated scene is used once per language version. They are realistic illustrative reconstructions, not archival evidence. Real archive images appear separately, with no false chronology implied for portraits.
